Frequently asked questions

The things customers and professionals ask us most. If your question isn't here, send us a message.

Booking

How do I book a professional?

Open the service page, choose Book now, pick one of the available dates and start times, and confirm. The booking is held for you immediately and the professional is asked to confirm it.

Can I book the same professional twice?

Yes, as long as you finish cancelling or completing the earlier booking. A slot can only hold one active booking, so overlapping times are shown as unavailable.

Why is a professional not in the search results?

Every professional profile is reviewed by our team before it becomes searchable. A profile that is still under review, paused by the professional, or suspended by an administrator will not appear in search — even if you follow a direct link to it.

How far ahead can I book?

Bookings open up to 60 days ahead and same-day bookings need at least 2 hours' notice. Slots come from each professional's own working hours and blocked dates.

Payment

How do I pay?

Cash only. There is no online payment on this platform and we never take card details. Agree the total before the work starts, then pay the professional in cash after the job is finished. The professional records the payment in the app, and you get a notification when they do.

What is the platform fee?

A small platform fee is added on top of the service price. It is shown in the checkout summary before you confirm, so the total is never a surprise.

What if the professional asks for more money?

Don't pay extra on the spot. Report it with the booking reference and we'll review the price that was agreed when you booked.

Changes and cancellations

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes, for free, as long as the booking has not started and you cancel at least 2 hours before the start time. Find the booking under My bookings and use Cancel booking.

The professional declined — what now?

The booking is closed and nothing is owed. Browse the service page for other available times, or pick a different professional.

The professional did not show up

Let us know through the booking page and contact support. We review the booking history before deciding what can be done.

Reviews

Can I review a booking?

Yes — once the professional marks the job complete. One review per booking, and the rating feeds directly into the average shown on the service and the professional's profile.

Can I edit or delete my review?

Reviews are part of the job history, so they can't be deleted. Contact support if a review contains personal or inappropriate content and we'll review it.

Professionals

How do I start offering services?

Register as a professional, create your profile with your trade and working area, and add at least one service. We review the profile before it goes live.

How do I set my working hours?

Under Schedule in your dashboard you set your regular weekly hours and block individual dates when you're unavailable. Bookable slots are generated from those hours.

When am I paid?

In cash, by the customer, after the job. Once they have paid, confirm it on the booking so your records match — that's the only "payout" step.

Account and privacy

Do I need to verify my email?

Yes. We send a 6-digit code when you register and you can't book or publish a profile until the address is verified.

How do I protect my account?

Use a unique password of at least 8 characters with a letter and a number. Repeated failed sign-ins lock the account temporarily, and you can reset your password from the login page with an emailed code.

Who can see my phone number?

Your number is never public. It is shown to a professional only for their own bookings, and only after the booking has been accepted.

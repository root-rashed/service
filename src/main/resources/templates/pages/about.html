About ServiZone

We connect neighbours with trusted local professionals — and keep the whole booking in one place, from first search to the finished job.

What we do

Searching for a reliable plumber, electrician, cleaner, tutor or photographer usually means asking around, comparing messages and hoping for the best. We put every professional in one searchable catalogue with real prices, real availability and reviews from customers who actually paid for the work.

Every professional creates a profile with their trade, experience, working area and skills. Our team reviews each profile before it goes live, and keeps checking that published work stays consistent with what customers report.

How we keep it trustworthy

  • Reviewed profiles. A professional is only searchable after an administrator has approved their profile.
  • Cash after the job. You agree the total before the work starts and pay the professional directly. We never handle your money.
  • Reviews from real bookings. Only customers who completed a booking can review it, once.
  • Real availability. Slots come from each professional's own working hours, so what you book is what they can do.
